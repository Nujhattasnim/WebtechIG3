PCMS Help

How can I use this page?

  • Enter the existing user's username to check their details.
  • Fill in the account information and select the appropriate role.
  • Enter the employee's employment and personal details.
  • Provide the identity details and upload the required documents.
  • Confirm that the information is accurate, then click Register User.
  • Click Clear Form to reset the fields.
  • Use Home or Login in the navigation bar to visit another page.
