About Our Services

  • Prisoner Management: Maintain organized records of prisoners, including personal details, crimes and sentence duration.
  • Cell Allocation: Assign and transfer prisoners to appropriate cells.
  • Medical Services: Maintain medical records, health checkups and prescription details.
  • Visitor Management: Register visitors and manage requests for prisoner visits.
  • Security Logs: Maintain records of system activities for security monitoring.
  • Staff Management: Register and manage system users and prison staff.

Why Choose PCMS?

  1. Role-based access for four different user categories
  2. Organized and centralized prison data management
  3. Improved monitoring of prison activities
  4. Efficient management of medical and visitor records
  5. A simple and user-friendly interface

About Our Services

  • Prisoner Management: Maintain complete records of all inmates including personal details, crimes, and sentence duration.
  • Cell Allocation: Assign and transfer prisoners to appropriate cells based on security levels.
  • Medical Services: Track inmate health records, medical checkups, and prescriptions.
  • Visitor Management: Allow family members and lawyers to request and schedule visits.
  • Security Logs: Monitor all system activities and maintain audit trails for security purposes.
  • Staff Management: Register and manage jailers, doctors, and administrative staff.

Why Choose PCMS?

  1. Role-based access for four different user categories
  2. Organized and centralized prison data management
  3. Improved security through activity logging
  4. Efficient handling of medical and visitor records
  5. Simple and user-friendly web interface
