Prison Cell Management System

Welcome to the Prison Cell Management System

The Prison Cell Management System (PCMS) is a web-based application designed to manage prison activities and information efficiently. The system aims to maintain organized records of prisoners, manage cell allocation, monitor medical services and handle visitor information.

PCMS provides access for four categories of users: Admin, Jailer, Prison Doctor and Visitor Manager. Each user has different responsibilities and can access the relevant features of the system according to their role.

About Our Services

  • Prisoner Management: Maintain organized records of prisoners, including personal details, crimes and sentence duration.
  • Cell Allocation: Assign and transfer prisoners to appropriate cells.
  • Medical Services: Maintain medical records, health checkups and prescription details.
  • Visitor Management: Register visitors and manage requests for prisoner visits.
  • Security Logs: Maintain records of system activities for security monitoring.
  • Staff Management: Register and manage system users and prison staff.

Why Choose PCMS?

  1. Role-based access for four different user categories
  2. Organized and centralized prison data management
  3. Improved monitoring of prison activities
  4. Efficient management of medical and visitor records
  5. A simple and user-friendly interface

PCMS Help

How can I use PCMS?

  • To add a new user, click "Add new User" under Admin.
  • To register a jailer, click "Register as Jailer".
  • To register a prisoner as a patient, click "Register a Prisoner as Patient".
  • To register a visitor, click "Register a Visitor".
  • To access your account, click Login in the navigation menu.
